FAQs

Frequently Ask Question

Find answers to common questions about our cleaning services and booking process.

What type of cleaning services do you offer? + −

We offer a wide range of cleaning services including regular house cleaning, deep cleaning, move-in/move-out cleaning, office cleaning, and more. You can choose the service that best fits your needs.

How do I book cleaning service? + −

Choose a service and preferred date in our booking form, then send us your details. Our team will contact you to confirm your appointment.

How much does cleaning service cost? + −

Pricing depends on the type of service, the size of your space, and any special requests. Contact us with your requirements for a tailored quote.

Do I need to provide cleaning supplies? + −

No. Our cleaners bring the equipment and products needed for your appointment. Let us know in advance if you have a preferred product or a special request.

What if I’m not satisfied with the service? + −

Please contact our support team and tell us what needs attention. We’ll work with you to make things right.

Can I reschedule or cancel my appointment? + −

Yes. Contact us as soon as possible and our team will help reschedule or cancel your appointment.
